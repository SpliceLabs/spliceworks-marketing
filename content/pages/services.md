# 4 places AI changes the company

One organization, one system. These surfaces share data, governance, and infrastructure — treating them as four separate initiatives fails.

---

## Build — AI development

How software gets created. We work inside your codebase, architecture, and review culture to redesign how software moves from intent to production, with deterministic gates protecting quality throughout.

---

## Embed — AI product

What customers experience. We design and build customer-facing AI inside the product flows people already use — copilots, retrieval, search, recommendations — grounded in your data and instrumented against real usage.

---

## Operate — AI operations

How the company gets work done. We separate the predictable path from work that needs judgment, then build governed AI into the systems already responsible for execution.

---

## Decide — AI decision systems

How the company thinks. We build systems around the questions your teams repeatedly need to answer: evidence, internal data, external research, and a defensible conclusion.

---

## Not sure which surface fits?

Start with a diagnostic. We map your situation and tell you which surface, or surfaces, you actually need.

# Bring us a workflow, not a brief

Tell us what's slow, expensive, or inconsistent today. We will tell you whether AI is the right instrument, and whether it belongs on Build, Embed, Operate, or Decide.

---

## Get in touch

Fill out the form below and we will respond within 2 business days.

### Required

- Name
- Email

### Optional

- Company
- Role
- Surface you'd start with (Build, Embed, Operate, Decide, not sure yet)
- Timeline
- The workflow that isn't working

---

## What happens next

1. We review your submission
2. We schedule a working call
3. We scope a diagnostic, or tell you honestly if AI isn't the right instrument
4. You decide whether to proceed

No sales pitch. Just a conversation about what you want to change.

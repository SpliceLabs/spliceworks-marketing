# Common questions

Answers to questions we hear most often.

---

## Services

### How long does an engagement take?

Depends on the surface and the work. A diagnostic typically runs 2-4 weeks. Deploy phases run 4-12 weeks. Operate and scale is ongoing.

### Do you work with specific industries?

We work with organizations that need AI systems deployed responsibly. Industry matters less than the problem you are solving.

### What if we already have AI systems running?

Start with a diagnostic. We will assess what you have and identify where governance and production-readiness are missing.

---

## Station and Hestia

### What is Splice Station?

Station is the governed platform underneath every engagement. By itself, it does nothing — it needs a configuration built for the work.

### What is Hestia?

Hestia is what Station becomes once we configure it for you. It is the backbone of your AI-native company: permissioned, audited, and yours to run after we leave.

### What is Hermes?

Hermes is our own engagement toolkit, used internally during diagnose and deploy work. It is not a product we hand to clients.

### Can we self-host Station?

Yes. Hosted, self-hosted, or hybrid deployments are all supported.

### Does Station work with existing agent frameworks?

Station is framework-agnostic. It orchestrates agents regardless of how they are built.

---

## Security

### Is Station SOC 2 compliant?

Hosted Station is SOC 2 Type II compliant. Self-hosted deployments inherit your compliance posture.

### Where does our data go?

Your data stays under your control. We define data boundaries before work begins.

### What about air-gapped deployments?

Self-hosted Station can run in air-gapped environments.

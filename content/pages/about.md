# We turn existing companies AI-native

Splice Works is the AI-transformation firm inside Splice Labs Group. We find where AI creates real leverage, build the systems that capture it, and stay accountable through production.

---

## Where we sit

Splice Labs Group owns the shared infrastructure, Splice Station, underneath everything we build. Splice Labs, a sibling company, is the venture studio that creates new AI-native companies from scratch, running Helios on Station to do it. Splice Works transforms companies that already exist, running Hermes internally and leaving Hestia behind as the client's own operating backbone.

---

## How we think about production AI

- We use AI to multiply senior engineering capacity. Our practitioners use Hermes and AI-native workflows for research, discovery, engineering, testing, and delivery.
- Splice, don't replace. Your ERP, CRM, codebase, and internal tools already encode how your company operates. We connect intelligence into them rather than starting a rewrite.
- Autonomy is earned. Systems start bounded. Evidence, consequence, and reversibility determine how much autonomy they receive.
- Governance is architecture. Identity, permissions, policy, evaluation, and audit are structural, not compliance work added later.
- Capability should compound. Every engagement leaves behind reusable patterns and people who know how to operate them.

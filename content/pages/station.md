# The governed platform underneath everything

Splice Station connects agent systems and external tools. Permissioning, audit trails, telemetry, security, and SSO in one control plane. By itself, Station does nothing — it needs a configuration built for the work.

---

## What gets deployed becomes your Hestia

Every Splice Works engagement runs a configuration on Station. What we build and leave behind is Hestia: the backbone of your AI-native company, hardened, permissioned, and yours to operate after the engagement ends.

---

## Run your agents

Station orchestrates agent execution. Define workflows, set triggers, monitor runs.

---

## Enforce approval gates

Gates block until approved. Automated checks, human reviews, or both. Nothing proceeds without passing.

---

## Track decisions and artifacts

Every decision has an owner. Every artifact is versioned. Evidence is required, not optional.

---

## Human and agent mode views

See what the agents see. Human mode shows outcomes. Agent mode shows capabilities and schemas.

---

## Run Station your way

### Hosted

We run Station for you. No infrastructure to manage.

- Managed updates
- 24/7 monitoring
- SLA-backed uptime

### Self-hosted

You run Station on your infrastructure. Full control.

- Your cloud or on-prem
- Custom security controls
- Air-gapped deployment option

### Hybrid

Split between hosted and self-hosted components.

- Control plane hosted
- Agents self-hosted
- Data stays on your infrastructure

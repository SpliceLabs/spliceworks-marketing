# Security is not optional

AI systems require rigorous controls. Every configuration we deploy on Splice Station is built with security from the start, not added afterward.

---

## Access controls

Role-based access. Principle of least privilege. Every permission is explicit.

---

## Audit trails

Every action is logged. Every decision is traceable. Nothing happens without a record.

---

## Compliance alignment

We work within your compliance requirements. SOC 2, GDPR, and HIPAA-aligned deployments.

---

## Data handling

Your data stays under your control. We define data boundaries before work begins.

---

## Gate enforcement

Critical operations require approval. Gates block until conditions are met.

---

## Evidence requirements

Decisions require evidence. Artifacts are versioned. The audit trail is the proof.

---

## Need a security review?

Start with a diagnostic. We evaluate your AI deployments for security and compliance gaps.

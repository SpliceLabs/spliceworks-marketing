# Source of Truth

## Company

**Splice Works** is the consulting and delivery arm of Splice Labs. We help organizations deploy AI-native systems — from assessment through operation.

- **Parent:** Splice Labs
- **Domain:** spliceworks.ai
- **Type:** Consulting + Deployment

## Offer

Five core services:

1. **Assessment** — Map your current state, identify high-value automation targets, define constraints
2. **Hardening** — Security review, compliance alignment, risk mitigation for AI deployments
3. **Delivery** — Build and ship AI-native workflows end-to-end
4. **Station Deployment** — Install and configure Splice Station as your AI control plane
5. **Operations** — Ongoing monitoring, maintenance, and evolution of deployed systems

## Product: Splice Station

Splice Station is the control plane. It orchestrates agents, enforces gates, tracks evidence, and keeps humans in control of AI operations.

- Runs your agents
- Enforces approval gates
- Tracks decisions and artifacts
- Provides Human/Agent mode views

## Delivery Stages

Every engagement follows four stages:

2. **Design** — Define architecture, plan implementation, set success criteria
3. **Deploy** — Build and ship the system with evidence and approvals
4. **Operate** — Monitor, maintain, and evolve deployed systems

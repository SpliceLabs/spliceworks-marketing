# Privacy Policy

SpliceOps (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through www.spliceops.ai, why, and what you can do about it.

## Who we are

SpliceOps is a brand owned and operated by Splice Labs Group, Inc., 19120 Sweet Clover Ln, Tampa, FL 33647, which is responsible for your data on this site. Questions or requests: {{contact_email}}.

## Information we collect

We may collect:

- Your name, work email, company, role, and the answers you give when you request a working session on [/book](/book) or email us.
- Device, browser, IP address, referral source, pages visited, and diagnostic information.
- Cookie and similar technology data.
- Calculator inputs, which are stored in the page URL and may be visible to our hosting providers or to anyone you share the link with.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through this website.

## How we use information

We may use personal information to:

- Respond to inquiries and prepare for and agree a working session.
- Operate, secure, and improve this website.
- Understand which companies visit, and follow up with relevant business outreach.
- Protect against fraud, misuse, and security incidents.
- Comply with legal obligations.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on legitimate interests, contract, legal obligation, and consent where required.

## Cookies and visitor identification

We use a small amount of local storage to remember your theme and reading mode. It never reaches our servers.

We use a visitor identification service to see which companies visit this site. It runs only after you choose Accept in the cookie banner. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as Decline. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer.

## Who we share it with

We may share information with:

- Hosting and infrastructure providers (Vercel).
- Email and business software providers (Resend, Google Workspace).
- Visitor identification providers (Gojiberry).
- Professional advisers, including lawyers and accountants.
- Government authorities where required by law.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## How long we keep it

We keep booking requests and emails for up to 24 months after our last substantive contact, or longer where we need them for an engagement or a legal or accounting duty. We keep visitor and business-contact data only as long as reasonably necessary for business outreach. If you opt out, we keep a suppression record so we don’t contact you again.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, loss, or misuse will never occur.

## International transfers

We are based in the United States, and some of our providers process data there and elsewhere. Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses. Ask us how a specific transfer is protected.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you.
- Object to or restrict certain processing, including direct marketing.
- Receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent where we rely on it.
- Complain to your data protection authority.

To use any of these, or to opt out of visitor identification, email {{contact_email}}. We reply within the time the law requires and may ask you to confirm who you are.

## Children

This website is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect children’s data. If you think a child sent us data, tell us and we will delete it.

## Third-party links

This website links to other sites we don’t control. This policy doesn’t cover them.

# Privacy notice

Last updated 3 Nov 2026.

This notice covers www.spliceops.ai. SpliceOps is the consulting arm of Splice Labs Group. Splice Labs Group, Inc., 19120 Sweet Clover Ln, Tampa, FL 33647, is responsible for your data on this site. Questions or requests: {{contact_email}}.

## What we collect

**When you book.** If you request a working session on [/book](/book), we receive what you enter: your name, work email and company, your website if you add it, your role, and your answers about your team, who asked for AI, where you are today and the workflow costing you most. You may also tell us anything else you want us to know, so please don't include anything you wouldn't want us to hold. We send the answers to our inbox by email.

**When you email us.** We receive your email address and what you write.

**What your browser sends.** Our host, Vercel, receives your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, as any host does.

**Visitor identification.** A script from Gojiberry loads on every page. See the next section.

**Stored on your device.** We save your theme and reading mode in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them. It never reaches our servers.

**Calculators.** The free tools run in your browser. Your inputs go into the page address so you can share a link. When someone opens a shared link, the address, including those inputs, reaches our host and Gojiberry like any page address. Don't put anything confidential into the calculators if you plan to share the link.

## Gojiberry

Gojiberry identifies visitors to a website. We use it to see which companies visit this site. We may then look for relevant business contacts at that company, who may not be the person who visited, and contact them with relevant business outreach.

According to Gojiberry's own policy, it collects your IP address, the pages you visit, where you came from and how long you stay. Its visitor identification runs on Snitcher, and results are shown at company level. It may set cookies or other identifiers in your browser. Read its policy at https://gojiberry.ai/privacy-policy. Gojiberry is operated by Superfruits SAS, Paris, France.

We don't yet ask for consent before this script loads. We are adding a consent choice for visitors in the EU, EEA and UK. Until then, you can opt out as described below.

## Why we use your data

- To reply to your booking request or email, and to prepare for and agree a working session.
- To keep the site working and secure.
- To remember your theme and mode.
- To identify companies that visit and follow up with business outreach.

Where the GDPR applies, our basis for replying to you and preparing a session is that you asked us to take steps before a contract. For security and outreach it is legitimate interests. Remembering your theme and mode is needed for a feature you chose.

## Who receives it

- Vercel, which hosts the site and runs the booking form.
- Resend, the email service that delivers your booking request to us. It acts on our behalf.
- Google Workspace, where we receive and store our email.
- Gojiberry, and the providers it uses, for visitor identification.
- Advisers, and authorities when the law requires it.

Some providers process data outside the UK and EEA, including in the United States. Resend processes mainly in the United States. It says it uses Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK addendum) and is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension (https://resend.com/legal/dpa). Ask us how any other transfer is protected.

## How long we keep it

Booking requests and emails: up to 24 months after our last substantive contact, or longer where we need them for an engagement or a legal or accounting duty, then we delete or anonymise them. Other data: only as long as we need it for the reasons above. Gojiberry sets its own retention. If you opt out, we keep your email address or company name on a list so we don't contact you again.

## Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, delete it, or stop using it. In the EU, EEA and UK you can also ask for restriction and portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests (including direct marketing), and withdraw consent where we rely on it. You can complain to your data protection authority. Residents of some US states may have additional rights under applicable law.

Email {{contact_email}}. We reply within the time the law requires. We may ask you to confirm who you are.

## Opt out of visitor identification

- Use Gojiberry's privacy choices: https://gojiberry.ai/optout.
- Email {{contact_email}} and we will ask Gojiberry to remove you and stop contacting you.
- Block the script in your browser. Most ad and tracker blockers do. The site works the same without it.

## Children

This site is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child sent us data, tell us and we will delete it.

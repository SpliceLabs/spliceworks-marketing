# Privacy notice

Last updated 3 Nov 2026.

This notice covers www.spliceops.ai. SpliceOps is the consulting arm of Splice Labs Group, and Splice Labs Group is responsible for your data on this site. Questions or requests: {{contact_email}}.

## What we collect

**What you send us.** If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. This site has no forms that send data to us.

**What your browser sends.** Our host, Vercel, receives your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, as any host does.

**Visitor identification.** A script from Gojiberry loads on every page. See the next section.

**Stored on your device.** We save your theme and reading mode in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them. It never reaches our servers.

**Calculators.** The free tools run in your browser. Your inputs go into the page address so you can share a link. We don't send them to a server. If you share the link, the inputs travel with it.

## Gojiberry

Gojiberry identifies visitors to a website. We use it to see which companies visit this site, and to follow up with relevant business outreach.

According to Gojiberry's own policy, it collects your IP address, the pages you visit, where you came from and how long you stay. Its visitor identification runs on Snitcher, and results are shown at company level. It may set cookies or other identifiers in your browser. Read its policy at https://gojiberry.ai/privacy-policy. Gojiberry is operated by Superfruits SAS, Paris, France.

We don't yet ask for consent before this script loads. We are adding a consent choice for visitors in the EU, EEA and UK. Until then, you can opt out as described below.

## Why we use your data

- To reply when you email us.
- To keep the site working and secure.
- To remember your theme and mode.
- To identify companies that visit and follow up with business outreach.

Where the GDPR applies, our basis for the email, security and outreach uses is legitimate interests. Remembering your theme and mode is needed for a feature you chose.

## Who receives it

- Vercel, which hosts the site.
- Gojiberry, and the providers it uses, for visitor identification.
- Advisers, and authorities when the law requires it.

Some providers process data outside the UK and EEA, including in the United States. Ask us how a specific transfer is protected.

## How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it for the reasons above, then we delete or anonymise it. Gojiberry sets its own retention. If you opt out, we keep your email address or company name on a list so we don't contact you again.

## Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, delete it, or stop using it. In the EU, EEA and UK you can also ask for restriction and portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests (including direct marketing), and withdraw consent where we rely on it. You can complain to your data protection authority. In California you can ask to know, correct and delete your personal information, opt out of sale or sharing, and you won't be treated worse for asking. We will honour Global Privacy Control signals once our consent choice is live.

Email {{contact_email}}. We reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm who you are.

## Opt out of visitor identification

- Use Gojiberry's privacy choices: https://gojiberry.ai/optout.
- Email {{contact_email}} and we will ask Gojiberry to remove you and stop contacting you.
- Block the script in your browser. Most ad and tracker blockers do. The site works the same without it.

## Children

This site is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child sent us data, tell us and we will delete it.

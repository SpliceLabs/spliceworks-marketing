If you're the founder and the whole team, start with the workflow that eats most of your week. We build a working version of it around you and AI. When we're done, you can run it yourself, and you stay accountable for what it does.

## ChatGPT every day, and the leads still by hand

"I'm the founder and the whole team. I use ChatGPT and Claude every day, but my leads, support and reporting still run by hand."

Chatting with AI speeds up a task you've started. Agents get the task done without you starting it, and closing that gap is the work we do with you.

## What you leave with

It's the same working session larger companies start with, sized to you:

- **A working prototype of one of your workflows.** Take inbound lead to booked meeting: a new lead comes in, agents research it, draft a reply and book the meeting, and you decide which accounts get a personal touch.
- A map of what to hand to agents next, in what order, and where you stay in the loop.
- **Where you are on the path**, and what the next step takes.

Most founders start at [AI-absent → AI-enabled](/services/ai-enabled). If you already run on AI every day, start at [AI-enabled → AI-native](/services/ai-native).

## One day, then you decide

1. **One working session.** We dig into how your business runs, then pick the workflow together.
2. **You decide where to stop.** Stop after the session, or have us take it further.
3. **No lock-in.** Bring your own agents or use ours, and either we run it for you or you run it yourself.

See [how it works](/how-it-works) for the full path.

## You stay in charge of the agents

You decide what they may approve on their own and what comes to you. Spend caps keep your AI bill in check, and a kill switch stops any agent at any time.

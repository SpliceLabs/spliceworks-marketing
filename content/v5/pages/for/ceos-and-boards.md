## The directive went out, and the work didn't move

- "The CEO said we're becoming an AI company. Nobody has defined what that means, or what we're allowed to automate."
- "The board asked for our AI plan. We have tools and no plan."
- "We rolled out Copilot and ChatGPT. Nothing about how the work gets done changed."

## Two steps, and you start where you are today

Becoming AI-native is the goal. We get you there one step at a time, and the working session tells you which step is yours.

|  | AI-absent → AI-enabled | AI-enabled → AI-native |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You're here if…** | You have the tools, maybe even daily users, but the work hasn't changed | Your first critical workflow runs on agents, but most work still waits to be asked |
| **What changes** | Your people work from one shared brain, and your first critical workflow is started and run by agents | That brain drives the work. Agents start it, do much of it, and escalate to your people based on your policies |
| **Read more** | [AI-absent → AI-enabled](/services/ai-enabled) | [AI-enabled → AI-native](/services/ai-native) |

Step 1 ends when your first workflow runs itself, with your people at the gates that matter. Step 2 does that for the workflows that run your business.

## What "done" looks like for the first workflow

Critical means it recurs, it matters to the business, and today someone starts it by hand. A one-off prompt or a demo doesn't count.

| Function | Agents start and run | A person decides at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sales | A new inbound lead comes in: agents research and enrich it, draft a reply and book the meeting | Outreach to strategic accounts |
| Support | A ticket comes in: agents triage it, resolve it from the brain if it's tier-1, or draft the reply | Refunds or credits above a set amount |
| Finance | An invoice arrives: agents match it to its purchase order and flag any exception | Payments and write-offs |

## Put AI to work. Keep people accountable.

Before anything runs, we agree who is accountable for each decision that matters, and what each agent may approve on their behalf. The system underneath has spend caps, a kill switch and full action logging. It also draws a clear line between what the AI controls and what it only watches.

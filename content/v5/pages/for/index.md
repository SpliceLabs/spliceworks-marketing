# Pick the page for the seat you're in

Leadership says the company will run on AI, and everyone has the tools, but the work still gets done the way it always has. What you need next depends on whether you issued the directive, were handed it, are the whole team, or back the companies that have it.

## Find your page

- **[CEOs and boards](/for/ceos-and-boards).** You issued the AI directive, or the board asked for the plan, and you want someone accountable for the results.
- **[Technology leaders](/for/technology-leaders).** For the CTO, CPO or Chief AI Officer who has to make it real and would rather not design the governance from scratch.
- **[Founders](/for/founders).** You're the whole team. You use AI every day; your leads, support and reporting are still manual.
- **[Accelerators](/for/accelerators).** For program leads who want every company in the cohort using AI safely.
- **[Investors](/for/investors).** You're at a VC, PE firm or family office. Every company you back has an AI mandate, and each is at a different step.

Looking by industry instead? See [manufacturing](/industries/manufacturing), [agencies](/industries/agencies) or [all industries](/industries).

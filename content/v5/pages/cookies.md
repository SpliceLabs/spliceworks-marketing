# Cookie Policy

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies we use on www.spliceops.ai, and how you can control them. It sits alongside our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. In this policy, “cookies” also covers similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, that remember something between visits.

## What we use

We use a small amount of local storage to make the site work the way you left it. These items stay in your browser and never reach our servers. We do not use advertising cookies.

| Name | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|
| `so-theme` | Remembers your Paper or Ink theme | Until you clear your browser data |
| `so-mode` | Remembers your reading mode | Until you clear your browser data |
| `so-consent` | Remembers your Accept or Decline choice | Until you clear your browser data |

## Visitor identification

We use a visitor identification service to see which companies visit this site. Its script loads in a waiting mode and does not start identifying visits until you choose Accept in the cookie banner. Once it starts, it may set cookies or other identifiers in your browser. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for what it collects and why.

## Your choices

- Choose Accept or Decline in the cookie banner.
- Change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer. If you withdraw a choice you gave earlier, the page reloads so the script stops.
- If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as Decline.
- Delete or block cookies in your browser settings, or use a tracker blocker. The site works the same without the visitor identification script.

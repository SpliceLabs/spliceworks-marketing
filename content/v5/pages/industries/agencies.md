# AI for agencies: agents take on the review work, your people decide

In an agency, the work that slows everything down is review. Every draft, creative and contract crosses the same few desks. We put agents on the review, and your people keep deciding what ships.

## The work piles up at the same few desks

- Every brief, draft, post and creative waits for a pass from the same senior people. The ones who know the brand voice best spend their days checking other people's work, giving the same notes again and again.
- An MSA or SOW sits untouched because only one person reads contracts closely, and they haven't had an afternoon to spare.
- **Your standards live in a few people's heads.** New hires learn them by getting things wrong.
- Everyone has ChatGPT, and reviews still happen the way they did before.

## How agents take on review

We learn how your agency reviews work today, then build that into a shared brain and agents that work inside the tools you already use.

### Review agents that know your standards

Your brand guides, client rules, contract positions and the notes your senior people always give, captured in one brain. Agents review against it and explain every flag, with its source.

### Agents that start the work

| Workflow | Agents start and run | A person decides at |
|---|---|---|
| Editorial review | A draft is submitted: agents check it against the client's voice and rules, then mark up the fixes | What goes to the client |
| Creative review | A creative is uploaded: agents check it against the brief and brand rules, then summarize the issues | Final sign-off on the creative |
| Contract review | Agents compare each incoming MSA or SOW with your standard terms and flag every deviation | Anything that changes your terms |

Your people decide what agents may approve, and they sign off on what goes to the client.

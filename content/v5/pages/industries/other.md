# AI for other industries: we start from your workflow, not your sector

We haven't built for your industry yet. We start from your workflow, and the working session is where we learn it. Our work so far is in manufacturing and agencies, and the method is the same in both: learn the work from the people doing it, build inside the systems you already use, and keep an accountable person behind every decision that matters.

**Book a working session** → /book

A paid day, credited in full if you go on to work with us.

## Signs it's a fit

- A few people hold the know-how, and everyone else waits for their answers.
- The same few desks review, approve or check the work, and the queue never empties.
- A wrong answer is expensive, so a confident guess isn't good enough.
- Your tools hold the work, but people still move it between them by hand.

If two or three of these sound like your team, bring us one workflow. It's the same shape of work we've done in manufacturing and agencies.

## How we start

1. **Pick one real workflow.** The one that hurts most, or the one your best people spend their week on.
2. **We learn it from the people doing it.** In your vocabulary: your product names, your exceptions, the notes everyone knows to give.
3. **You leave with a working prototype.** Once we've seen your workflow, we tell you how long it will take to reach production.

## Questions we get

### Who's accountable when the AI gets it wrong?

A person on your side. There's an accountable person behind every decision that matters, and agents can handle some approvals that person has permissioned. Before anything runs, we agree who is accountable and what each agent may approve.

### How fast will we see something?

You get something usable from the first session. Once we've seen your workflow there, we tell you how long it will take to reach production.

### What does it cost?

Talk to us. We scope the work with you during the working session, which is credited in full if you go on to work with us.

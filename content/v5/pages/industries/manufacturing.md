# AI for manufacturing that starts with what your experts know

In a lot of manufacturers, the answers about the products live with one or two specialists. We capture what they know and put it to work in sales and support. There's an accountable person behind every decision that matters.

## Your best product knowledge lives in one or two heads

- A customer asks whether a product will hold up in their process. The question sits in a specialist's inbox while the rep and the customer both wait, and the specialist spends another week answering questions instead of improving the product.
- **Matching a product to a use case takes a specialist.** When they're busy, the match is a guess, or it doesn't happen.
- One wrong promise costs more than a slow answer. A rep says a product can do something it can't, and the company has to walk it back.
- New reps learn the catalog by asking the same overloaded people. When a specialist is out, questions wait until they're back. When one leaves, much of what they knew leaves too.

If you've tried a chatbot on the product sheets, you've seen it answer confidently and sometimes wrongly, with no way to tell which.

## What we build with your specialists

The work starts with your specialists, and everything we build lives inside the systems you already use.

### A product brain built from your specialists

We sit with the people who know your products and write down how they think: which product fits which use case, where each one stops working, and the exceptions they carry around in their heads. That goes into one shared brain your people can ask. Every answer shows where it came from.

### Answers that refuse what you can't stand behind

The brain matches products to use cases and won't promise what a product can't do. If a question goes beyond what your specialists have confirmed, it says so and hands the question to a person.

### Agents that start the work

Once people trust the brain, agents stop waiting to be asked. A few first workflows we might build together:

| Workflow | Agents start and run | A person decides at |
|---|---|---|
| Technical inquiry | An inquiry arrives: agents find the right product in the brain, draft the answer and cite the source | Anything outside a product's confirmed uses |
| Inbound lead | A new lead comes in: agents research and enrich it, draft a reply and book the meeting | Outreach to strategic accounts |
| Sales call prep | Agents research the buyer and prepare a tailored deck for the rep before the meeting | What's promised to the customer |

Your specialists set what the agents may answer and approve, and they stay accountable for it.

A person on your side. There's an accountable person behind every decision that matters, and agents can handle some approvals that person has permissioned. Before anything runs, we agree who is accountable and what each agent may approve.

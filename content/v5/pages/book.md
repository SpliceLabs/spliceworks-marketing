# Book a working session

We look at how your work runs, pick one real workflow with you and build a working version of it. Leave with a decision, not a deck.

**The session has its own fee, agreed with you before you book. It's credited in full against your engagement if you go ahead.**

## What you walk away with

- **A working prototype** on one of your real workflows.
- **The workflow, mapped:** how it runs today, and where people and agents fit.
- **Where you are on the path:** AI-absent, AI-enabled or AI-native, and your first steps from there.
- **A build, buy or partner call** for each part of that workflow.
- **Who is accountable** for each decision that matters, including what agents may approve.
- **Our advice** on where to go next.

## One focused day

The session takes one day, or the same eight working hours spread out if that suits your team.

### Who should be in the room

- **Whoever issued the AI directive:** the CEO, a board member, or you, the founder. We spend the first hours with them.
- **Whoever owns making it real:** often the COO, head of operations, CTO or CIO.
- The experts who do the work today, whose knowledge the workflow depends on.

At a small company, that can be one person. The session is the same shape for a solo founder as for a large team: we pick one workflow of yours, for example inbound lead to booked meeting, and you leave with a working prototype and a map of what to automate next.

### Booking for a portfolio or a program?

Choose that on the form and tell us a little about it. We'll show you what a session looks like for one of your companies.

## What it costs

There are two separate prices here, and they're settled at different times.

- **The session fee** covers the working session. We agree it with you, along with the terms, before anything is booked.
- **The engagement** is the work after the session, if you go ahead. We scope and price it with you during the session, once we've seen the workflow. The session fee is credited in full against it.

## Before you book

### What does it cost?

Talk to us. We scope the work with you during the working session, which is credited in full against your engagement.

### Is our data safe?

Your data stays in the systems you already use. We work inside them, with only the access you give us, under NDA.

## Who you'll work with

You'll work with Tim Varner, Jake Krogman and our forward-deployed team. Station, the system we'd configure for you, runs Splice every day. [See our work](/work)

## What happens next

1. **We reply.** We usually respond within 24 hours.
2. **Details agreed.** The session fee and terms, the date, who should be in the room, and an NDA.
3. **We hold the session.** A day on one of your workflows.
4. **You decide.** You leave with a prototype, our advice and a scoped next step, and you choose whether to go ahead.

## Tell us where you are

A few questions so we come prepared and bring the right people. Questions marked * are required.

1. **This session is for…** *
   - My company
   - Companies in my portfolio (VC, PE or family office)
   - Founders in my accelerator or program
   - Someone else's company (I'm introducing them)
2. **Your role** *
   - Founder
   - CEO or COO
   - Board member
   - CTO, CPO, CIO or Chief AI Officer
   - Head of operations or another business lead
   - Investor or operating partner
   - Program lead
   - Other
3. **Who asked for AI?** *
   - The CEO
   - The board
   - Me, the founder
   - Our investors
   - No one yet: we're getting ahead of it
4. **Who owns making it real?** *
   - Me
   - Someone else: name and role
   - No one yet
5. **Where are you today?** *
   - We have the tools, maybe even daily users, but the work hasn't changed
   - One critical workflow runs on agents, but most work still waits to be asked
   - We need someone to own AI on our leadership team first
   - Not sure yet: the session will place us
6. **What made you look now?** Pick any.
   - The CEO said we're becoming an AI company, but nobody has defined what that means
   - The board asked for our AI plan
   - We rolled out Copilot or ChatGPT and nothing changed
   - We ran pilots; none reached production
   - What our best people know lives in one or two heads
   - We can't let AI act on its own until we know who's accountable
   - I'm the founder and the whole team, and my business still runs by hand
   - Something else: tell us
7. **Which workflow is costing you most?** One line is enough. (optional)
8. **For a portfolio or program: roughly how many companies?** 1–5 / 6–20 / More than 20
9. **Your name** *, **Work email** *, **Company** *, **Website** (optional)
10. **Anything we should know before we reply?** (optional)

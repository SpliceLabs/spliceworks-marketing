# A governed AI workspace where your people and agents work

Our delivery runs on one system. It gives your agents room to do real work and keeps an accountable person behind every decision that matters. We call it Station.

## What it does for you

You set the controls:

- What agents may do on their own, what they escalate, and to whom. Agents may approve only what an accountable person has permissioned.
- Which actions the AI takes and which it only observes. We state that line plainly.
- Spend caps that keep agents inside the limits you set, and a kill switch that stops them at any time.

## Your agents or ours

Bring your own agents or use ours, and swap them at any time. Station works in the systems you already run.

## Configured for your work

We configure Station for your workflows, your policies and your people, as part of [each step on the path](/services).

## Hosting options

We run it for you by default. You can also self-host it.

# How ready is your company for AI?

Most companies have AI tools by now. Fewer have changed how the work gets done. Answer twelve questions, in about five minutes, to see where you are on the path from AI-absent to AI-native, and whether your foundations back that up. No sign-up: your answers stay in your browser.

## How the check-up works

It asks how far AI goes in four areas of your company:

- **Core work**, what you make or deliver: 45% of the score
- **Customers**, meaning sales, marketing and support: 25%
- **Back office**, meaning finance, people and admin: 15%
- **Management**, meaning reporting, planning and decisions: 15%

Each area sits on one of five rungs: AI isn't part of the work, it helps people, it does steps, it runs the workflow, or it improves the workflow.

Eight more questions check the foundations that hold a rung up: how many of your desk-based people use AI, who owns it, what it knows about your business, how it reaches your systems, how its work is checked, whether you measure what it changes, what rules cover it, and whether any workflow actually runs differently.

We count a rung only as far as your foundations hold it up. "Does steps" needs your foundations to average 3.5 out of 5, "runs the workflow" needs 4, and "improves the workflow" needs 4.5 and real measurement. Where an area goes further than that, we count the rung your foundations support. It's a short form of the method we use when we assess a team. The check-up runs in your browser and doesn't store or send your answers.

## Where you can land

- **AI-absent:** you may have AI tools, and people may use them every day, but the work runs the way it always has. If AI already does steps of the work in one area, you're close to AI-enabled.
- **AI-enabled:** at least one workflow is started and run by agents, with your people deciding at the points that matter.
- **AI-native:** agents run the workflows that run your business, your foundations back that up, and you score 4 out of 5 or more.

## A worked example

A firm says AI does steps in its core work and in support, helps people in finance, and isn't part of management reporting. Its foundations average about 2.6: people use AI, but only informal champions own it and nothing is measured. Those foundations back AI helping people, not doing steps, so two areas go further than their foundations support. The result is AI-absent, 1.9 out of 5.

Give AI a part-time owner, estimate what it changes, set rules and change one workflow, and the foundations average about 3.6. Both areas then count at "does steps", and the firm is close to AI-enabled.

## What the check-up leaves out

It's a quick read, not an audit. It takes your answers at face value, uses the same eight foundations as evidence for every area, and doesn't look at your systems, data or people. Bring your result to a working session and we'll test it on one of your real workflows.

## Questions about AI readiness

### How do I know if my company is ready for AI?

Look at whether the work has changed, not whether people have tools. Pick one recurring workflow and ask how far AI goes in it: does it help people, do steps, or start and run the workflow with people deciding at set points? Then check the foundations: an owner, AI that knows your business, a way to check its work, and a way to measure what changes. [Take the check-up](/tools/ai-check-up).

### What's the difference between AI-enabled and AI-native?

AI-enabled means at least one critical workflow is started and run by agents, with your people deciding at the points that matter. AI-native means that's how the company runs: agents run the workflows that run your business.

### Why ask about foundations as well as AI use?

Because a claim needs something to hold it up. If agents run a workflow but nobody owns AI and nothing is measured, the workflow is hard to trust and hard to improve. The check-up counts the rung your foundations support, not the one you claim.

### What should we fix first?

The foundation that holds you back most, which is rarely another tool. If nobody owns AI, start there. If AI's work isn't checked or measured, people won't trust it to run a workflow. Then pick one workflow and change how it runs.

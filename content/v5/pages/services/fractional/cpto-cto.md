# A fractional CPTO or CTO to make your product AI-native

Your customers, or your competitors, are starting to expect a product that agents can use and that does more on its own. This seat owns that: the roadmap, a product surface agents can work with, and the first AI-native feature in production. It sits on your leadership team part-time, without a full-time hire.

## Who this seat is for

- AI-native products are starting to pull your roadmap.
- Your product can't yet be used by an agent on a customer's behalf.
- You have ideas for AI features and no one accountable for shipping the first.
- You lead the company or the product and don't have a technical executive for this.

## What the seat owns

- An AI-native product roadmap
- An agent-ready product surface live
- The first AI-native feature in production, against an adoption target
- A written mandate for the permanent role, if you want one

We start from this list and pick what fits your product. The split with your own leaders is on the [fractional leadership page](/services/fractional).

## The first quarter, in the order we usually work

1. Agree the one-page success plan: mandate, outcomes with baselines, and checkpoints.
2. Set the AI-native roadmap with your product and engineering leads.
3. Make the product surface ready for agents.
4. Put the first AI-native feature in production, against an adoption target.
5. Hold the quarterly review: renew, expand into an engagement, or hand over.

## Beside your product and engineering leads

The seat adds the AI mandate to your product leadership. It doesn't replace the people running your product and your systems today. For the company-wide AI mandate, see the [Chief AI Officer seat](/services/fractional/chief-ai-officer).

## Questions about the seat

### What is the difference between the seat and an AI consultant?

A consultant delivers recommendations and leaves. The seat sits on your leadership team and answers for outcomes you agree in writing each quarter, including the ones that need your people to change how they work.

### How does a fractional seat end?

The end is written into the plan from the first quarter. At each quarterly review you renew, expand into an engagement, or hand over: to a permanent hire we help you define, to an executive you already have, or to advisory.

### When should we hire a full-time AI executive instead?

When AI is your product and decisions on it come every day, or when the role has to hold headcount and a budget of its own. If that's you, we'll say so, and we can write the mandate for the hire.

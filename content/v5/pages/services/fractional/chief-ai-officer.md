# A fractional Chief AI Officer, with outcomes in writing

Your teams are using AI, and nobody answers for the whole of it: the plan, the spend, who decides what. The Chief AI Officer seat is the person who does. It sits on your leadership team part-time, without a full-time hire, and answers for outcomes you agree each quarter.

## Who this seat is for

- Several teams buy AI tools, and nobody holds the whole list or the bill.
- Pilots run, then stop before production.
- Your board or investors ask about AI, and you want one answer.
- You can't yet say who may let an agent act, and on what.
- You aren't sure what a permanent AI role should look like.

## What the seat owns

- An AI plan and mandate approved by your CEO or board
- An inventory of your AI tools and contracts, with the waste cut
- An AI spend policy
- The accountability map in force: who decides what, and what agents may approve
- The first critical workflow started and run by agents
- A written mandate for the permanent role, if you want one

We start from this list and pick what fits your company. The split with your own leaders is on the [fractional leadership page](/services/fractional).

## The first quarter, in the order we usually work

1. Agree the one-page success plan: mandate, outcomes with baselines, and checkpoints.
2. Take stock of your AI tools, contracts and spend.
3. Put the spend policy and the accountability map in force.
4. Start the first critical workflow with agents.
5. Hold the quarterly review: renew, expand into an engagement, or hand over.

## Beside your COO, CIO and CTO

Many companies reach for AI to streamline operations, and that work sits with your COO. The seat doesn't replace them. Your COO keeps operations, and the seat works beside them on where AI changes how the work runs. Your CIO and CTO keep the systems. If you lead technology yourself, see [our page for technology leaders](/for/technology-leaders).

## Questions about the seat

### What is the difference between the seat and an AI consultant?

A consultant delivers recommendations and leaves. The seat sits on your leadership team and answers for outcomes you agree in writing each quarter, including the ones that need your people to change how they work.

### Does the seat replace our COO, CIO or CTO?

No. Your COO keeps operations, and your CIO or CTO keeps the systems. The seat owns the AI mandate beside them: the plan, the spend, who decides what and the first results.

### How does a fractional seat end?

The end is written into the plan from the first quarter. At each quarterly review you renew, expand into an engagement, or hand over: to a permanent hire we help you define, to an executive you already have, or to advisory.

### When should we hire a full-time AI executive instead?

When AI is your product and decisions on it come every day, or when the role has to hold headcount and a budget of its own. If that's you, we'll say so, and we can write the mandate for the hire.

# Start where your company is today

Becoming AI-native is the goal. We get you there in two steps, each ending in a milestone you can see. Step 1 proves it on one critical workflow. Step 2 makes it how your company runs. If you're not sure which step fits, the working session places you.

## Three stages, in plain words

- **AI-absent:** tools, maybe, but nothing about how the work gets done has changed.
- **AI-enabled:** your people work from one shared brain, and your first critical workflow is started and run by agents.
- **AI-native:** that brain drives the work, and agent-run workflows are how the company operates.

## Pick the step that fits

| | [AI-absent → AI-enabled](/services/ai-enabled) | [AI-enabled → AI-native](/services/ai-native) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You're here if | You have the tools, maybe even daily users, but the work hasn't changed | Your first critical workflow runs on agents, but most work still waits to be asked |
| We build | Your company's brain, a governed workspace, and your first agent-run workflow | Critical workflows rebuilt so the brain drives the work, with agents as coworkers that escalate by your policies |
| It ends when | Your first critical workflow is started and run by agents | The workflows that run your business, started and run by agents |
| Not for | Companies that want to buy tools without changing how they work; companies without an executive sponsor | Companies that don't yet run a critical workflow on agents; companies that want agents acting with no person accountable |

Both steps keep an accountable person behind every decision that matters, and both work inside the systems you already run.

## Gaps no competitor fills (agent comparison, my reading)

- None combines tested tools with spec sheets, case studies, event analysis and a newsletter on one surface.
- None gives a non-sales reader path (subscribe, archive, search) next to the demo CTA, except Tenex Ultrathink off-site.
- None segments by the reader's AI maturity.
- Tenex's homepage says it shifts companies "from AI-absent to AI-native", which is close to our own framing. We differentiate on substance (tested, dated, sourced, accountable), not on framing.

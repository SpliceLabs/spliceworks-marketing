# AI check-up copy review

## Consensus (all three)

1. **Q8 (systems access) is the drop-off.**
   - "Broad keys", "scoped access" and "audit trail" are jargon. A CEO or COO can't answer it without asking IT.
   - "Shared accounts or broad keys" sits second in the list, so it reads as progress, even though it scores 1 and is the riskiest option.
   - There is no answer for AI built into tools the company already pays for, such as Copilot or the CRM.
2. **Area rungs 1 and 2 blur together.** "Chat with AI on their own" and "drafts, summarises and researches" describe the same habit. "Agents" in rungs 4 and 5 is never defined.
3. **Remove "markdown" from the AI-absent next step.** It reads as a tools class. Keep the basics offer but say it in the buyer's words.
4. **Remove "generalised from the agentic maturity model in our team assessment".** All three call it insider language.
5. **Q10: "baseline" is jargon.** Q9: "before it counts" is vague.
6. **Q6 needs a home for a named but part-time owner.** That is where most firms sit, and they will round up to "Someone, part-time".

## Two of three

- **The "close to AI-enabled" meaning line contradicts the answers** (copywriter, product marketer). They said AI does steps, and the line replies "you have AI tools, or a mandate to get them".
- **The gaps line says "claim"** (COO, product marketer). It sounds like an accusation of exaggerating at the moment they decide whether to book.
- **"Credited in full against your engagement"** (copywriter, product marketer). It assumes an engagement the buyer hasn't agreed to and doesn't say the day is paid.
- **Q7's middle option says "connected"** (copywriter, product marketer). It sounds like an IT project. Uploading files to a project or keeping shared notes is the real step before that, and those buyers currently score as paste-in.
- **Q12 says "didn't reach production"** (copywriter, product marketer). "Production" means a factory floor to a manufacturer.
- **Q11 has no answer for "draft or informal guidance"** (copywriter, COO).

## Single-reviewer points worth taking

- **Q5 counts the whole headcount** (product marketer). A firm with shop-floor, field or care staff scores 1 even when its office uses AI weekly. That caps every area. Fix: ask about people whose work is mostly documents, email or a computer.
- **The method paragraph leaves out the 3.5 threshold for "does steps"** (copywriter), so the page doesn't fully describe the scoring.

## Rejected or deferred

- **COO: make the AI-absent next step about workflow and rules.** This conflicts with the founder decision to start AI-absent companies on the basics. Kept the basics and reworded them instead.
- **COO: five options on Q6, Q10 and Q11.** This changes the scoring and adds friction. Deferred: the wording fixes reduce rounding up without new values.
- **COO: shorten or cut the worked example.** The product marketer says keep it. Kept.
